# SEE diagrams and term navigation

The dictionary now selects a visual representation by exact term name. `diagrams.js` handles rendering, court positions, basic actions, step controls and playback. `motion-examples.js` contains additional teaching examples. These are schematic illustrations, not scale drawings or complete team playbooks.

- 357 of 514 records currently receive a court, movement, footwork or shooting illustration. Some related terms share a clearly labeled common-principle illustration. A single static formation is not presented as a complete set play.
- 157 records receive a concept guide. This includes rules, statistics, teaching concepts and movements/variants without a sufficiently specific visual model. These sections say “SEE｜ポイントを整理”; they do not claim to animate those movements.
- Solid lines describe player travel, blue dotted lines dribbling, orange dashed lines passes. Attackers are circles, defenders rounded squares; ball possession is marked in orange. Footwork and side-view shooting have their own legends.
- Playback advances through explanation frames and stops at the end. It does not imply biomechanically accurate animation. Moving to another term, returning to the list, opening a quiz or selecting a category stops playback.
- Category selection clears the selected term, query and optional filters, restores the list, and ends an active quiz through the existing learning persistence mechanism.
- Previous/next use the same filtering and ordering as the displayed list, and disable at its ends.

Reference material consulted for the basic screen/cut distinctions:
- FIBA/WABC coaching resources: https://about.fiba.basketball/en/wabc-documents
- FIBA Europe Basketball Terminology: https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/v1727441380/documents-corporate-regional-offices-europe-programs-fiba-europe-coaching-certificate-fiba-europe-basketball-terminology.pdf

The existing dictionary's AI-content notice still applies. The illustrations are locally authored SVG, not copied source diagrams.

Validation: model integrity for every dictionary record; browser checks for category reset, filtered list navigation, step switching, representative desktop/mobile visual captures, quiz feedback, audio playback and learning synchronization.
